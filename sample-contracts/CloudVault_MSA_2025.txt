MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2025
between Acme Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of
Maharashtra, India ("Client"), and CloudVault Solutions Ltd., a company incorporated
under the laws of Karnataka, India ("Service Provider").

1. TERM AND RENEWAL

1.1 This Agreement commences on January 15, 2025 and continues for a period of
twelve (12) months, expiring on January 14, 2026.

1.2 AUTO-RENEWAL: This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year
terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30 (thirty)
days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Notice must be delivered by
registered post or email with read receipt.

1.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing 90 (ninety)
days' written notice to the other party.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

2.1 The Client shall pay a monthly service fee of INR 85,000 (Rupees Eighty-Five
Thousand) on the 1st day of each calendar month, beginning February 1, 2025.

2.2 Late payments shall attract interest at 18% per annum, calculated daily.

2.3 All invoices must be raised by the Service Provider on or before the 25th of
each preceding month. Failure to raise invoice by the 25th shall not relieve the
Client of payment obligation.

2.4 ANNUAL PRICE REVIEW: Service fees shall be subject to review on January 1, 2026
with a maximum escalation of 10% per annum. Service Provider must give 60 days
written notice of any fee change.

3. SERVICE LEVELS

3.1 The Service Provider guarantees 99.5% uptime for all core services, measured
monthly. Scheduled maintenance windows (maximum 4 hours per month, between
02:00 and 06:00 IST) are excluded from uptime calculations.

3.2 SLA REVIEW: Service level performance shall be formally reviewed on a quarterly
basis. First review scheduled for April 1, 2025. Subsequent reviews: July 1, 2025;
October 1, 2025; January 1, 2026.

3.3 If uptime falls below 99.5% in any calendar month, Client is entitled to a
service credit of 10% of that month's fee. Credits must be claimed within 15 days
of month-end. Credits shall not exceed 30% of monthly fees.

4. DATA AND SECURITY

4.1 ANNUAL AUDIT: The Service Provider shall submit to an independent security audit
once per year, with the first audit to be completed by December 1, 2025. Audit
reports shall be made available to Client within 7 days of completion.

4.2 The Service Provider shall maintain ISO 27001 certification throughout the term
of this Agreement. Proof of certification renewal must be provided to Client by
November 15, 2025.

4.3 In the event of a data breach, the Service Provider shall notify the Client within
72 hours of discovery. Failure to notify within this window constitutes a material
breach.

5. LIABILITY

5.1 The Service Provider's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed
the total fees paid in the 3 months immediately preceding the event giving rise
to the claim.

5.2 The Client's liability for any cause of action is uncapped and shall include
all direct, indirect, incidental and consequential damages.

5.3 INDEMNIFICATION: Client shall indemnify and hold harmless Service Provider from
any third-party claims arising from Client's use of the services.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of each other's proprietary
information for a period of 3 years following termination of this Agreement.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Maharashtra, India.

7.2 All disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at
Mumbai, Maharashtra.

7.3 Prior to litigation, parties agree to attempt mediation for a period of 30 days.

8. PENALTIES

8.1 If Service Provider fails to meet the 99.5% SLA for 3 consecutive months,
Client may terminate immediately without penalty.

8.2 If Client terminates before the end of the initial 12-month term without cause,
Client shall pay an early termination fee equal to 3 months' service fees
(INR 2,55,000).

- Monthly Payments Due:     1st of each month (Feb 2025 – Jan 2026)
- Q1 SLA Review:            April 1, 2025
- Q2 SLA Review:            July 1, 2025
- Q3 SLA Review:            October 1, 2025
- Q4 SLA Review / Renewal:  January 1, 2026
- Security Audit Due:       December 1, 2025
- ISO Cert Renewal Proof:   November 15, 2025
- Annual Price Review:      January 1, 2026
