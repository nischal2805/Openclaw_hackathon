SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Software as a Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2025
between Nexus Retail Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of Delhi, India
("Customer"), and DataSync Technologies Inc., a corporation incorporated under the laws
of Delaware, United States of America ("Provider").

1. SUBSCRIPTION TERM

1.1 This Agreement commences on March 1, 2025 and shall remain in effect for a period
of twenty-four (24) months, expiring on February 28, 2027.

1.2 AUTO-RENEWAL: Unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least
15 (fifteen) days prior to the end of the then-current subscription term, this Agreement
shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods at the then-current list price
plus a standard 12% annual escalation.

1.3 Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 120 (one hundred and
twenty) days' written notice. Provider may terminate for convenience upon 30 (thirty)
days' written notice. Early termination by Customer shall attract a termination fee equal
to 50% of remaining subscription fees for the unexpired term.

2. SUBSCRIPTION FEES AND BILLING

2.1 Customer shall pay a quarterly subscription fee of USD 12,500 (Twelve Thousand Five
Hundred US Dollars) in advance, on the first business day of each quarter beginning
April 1, 2025.

2.2 Invoices not paid within 15 days of due date shall attract a late payment fee of
2% per month (compounded monthly), plus all costs of collection including reasonable
attorney fees.

2.3 PRICE REVIEW: Provider reserves the right to revise subscription fees at any time
with 30 (thirty) days' written notice to Customer. Customer's continued use of the
service after the notice period constitutes acceptance of the new pricing.

2.4 All fees are exclusive of taxes. Customer is solely responsible for all applicable
taxes, duties, and levies including GST, withholding tax, and any cross-border
transaction taxes.

3. SERVICE LEVELS AND SUPPORT

3.1 Provider guarantees 99.0% monthly uptime for the core DataSync platform. Uptime
excludes scheduled maintenance windows of up to 8 hours per month and any downtime
caused by Customer's systems or third-party integrations.

3.2 SLA REVIEW: Uptime performance and support ticket resolution metrics shall be
reviewed semi-annually. Review meetings to be held on September 1, 2025 and
March 1, 2026.

3.3 In the event of SLA breach, Customer's sole remedy shall be a service credit of 5%
of the affected month's quarterly fee (pro-rated). Total credits shall not exceed 10% of
annual subscription fees. Provider shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental,
consequential, or punitive damages arising from SLA failures.

3.4 SUPPORT: Provider shall provide email-only support during US business hours
(9 AM – 6 PM EST, Monday to Friday). Response time targets: P1 critical — 8 business
hours; P2 high — 3 business days; P3 medium — 10 business days.

4. DATA AND SECURITY

4.1 AUDIT RIGHT: Customer shall have the right to request one (1) security audit per
calendar year with a minimum of 60 days' written notice. Provider may, at its sole
discretion, substitute a third-party SOC 2 Type II report in lieu of a direct audit.
Annual audit window: January 2026.

4.2 DATA PORTABILITY: Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement, Provider shall
make Customer's data available for export for a period of 30 (thirty) days following
the termination date. After this 30-day window, Provider shall permanently delete all
Customer data with no obligation to retain or restore.

4.3 Provider processes Customer data in data centres located in the United States and
the European Union. Customer acknowledges and consents to cross-border data transfers
subject to Provider's standard Data Processing Addendum.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND LIABILITY

5.1 All intellectual property rights in the DataSync platform remain exclusively with
Provider. This Agreement grants Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable
licence to access the platform during the subscription term only.

5.2 LIMITATION OF LIABILITY: Provider's aggregate liability to Customer for any and
all claims arising under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the
total fees paid by Customer in the 3 (three) months immediately preceding the claim.

5.3 CUSTOMER LIABILITY: Customer's liability to Provider for any breach of this
Agreement, including unauthorised use, data misuse, or non-payment, shall be
UNLIMITED and shall include all direct, indirect, consequential, and punitive damages.

5.4 INDEMNIFICATION: Customer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider
from any and all third-party claims arising from Customer's use of the platform,
Customer's data, or Customer's violation of applicable law, without limitation.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other party's Confidential
Information for the duration of this Agreement and for a period of 5 (five) years
following termination.

6.2 POST-TERMINATION DATA RETURN: Customer must notify Provider within 14 (fourteen)
days of termination whether it requires a data export. Failure to notify within this
window shall be deemed a waiver of data portability rights.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
the State of Delaware, United States of America, without regard to its conflict of
law provisions.

7.2 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively by binding
arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association in New York, USA.
Customer waives any right to bring claims in Indian courts or under Indian law.

7.3 NOTICE DEADLINES: All notices under this Agreement must be delivered by email with
read receipt confirmed. Any claim for service credits or SLA breaches must be submitted
within 15 days of the end of the affected service month. Claims submitted after this
deadline shall be deemed waived.

8. INSURANCE

8.1 Each party shall maintain, at its own expense, adequate insurance coverage including
commercial general liability insurance of not less than USD 2,000,000 per occurrence.
Customer must provide evidence of coverage to Provider by April 1, 2025 and annually
thereafter on March 1 of each year.

8.2 Customer shall name Provider as an additional insured on all liability policies.
